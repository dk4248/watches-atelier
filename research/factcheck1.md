IDENTITY VERDICT FACT-CHECK: NOMOS Tangente ref. 139 / "DUW 4001" (checked 2026-10-15)

BOTTOM LINE
- The user's naming is correct for a ref. 139 bought new today. "DUW 4001" is a real, current NOMOS caliber, and NOMOS's live ref. 139 page names it.
- Older ref. 139s carry the Alpha. Those built from about 2023 already had the DUW regulation system, not the Triovis.
- No primary source dates the Alpha to DUW 4001 switch for ref. 139. "From about 2025" is an inference.
- The one part of the identity section I partly refute: Alpha-era 139s are not uniformly "Triovis + silver Nivarox hairspring". The blue-vs-silver hairspring test is not conclusive. The plate engraving is the test to rely on.

RECOMMENDED CORRECTION TO THE SPEC'S IDENTITY ROW
"Ref. 139 currently ships with DUW 4001: swing system, blue spring, DUW regulation, 53 h, plate engraved DUW 4001 / DEUTSCHE UHRENWERKE / 17 RUBINE. Earlier 139s use the Alpha (43 h, 17 STEINE, NOMOS GLASHÜTTE; α serial on standard Alphas). On Alphas, the Triovis regulator was replaced by DUW regulation from about 2023, which NOMOS confirmed in April 2023. The cut-over to DUW 4001 is ≈2025 and undocumented. Identify by the plate engraving first; hairspring colour and regulator type are secondary."
