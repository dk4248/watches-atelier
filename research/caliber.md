NOMOS GLASHÜTTE TANGENTE REF. 139: CALIBER DUW 4001 RESEARCH REPORT (with a short note on caliber Alpha)

Conventions used throughout:
- "Back view" means looking through the sapphire case back, with 12 o'clock up. The crown is then on the LEFT at 9 o'clock.
- Coordinates are in mm from the movement centre (the centre-wheel arbor). In back view, x+ is to the right and y+ is towards 12.
- To get dial-side coordinates, use x_dial = −x_back; y is unchanged.
- "Clock" means the position as seen in back view.

=====================================================================
0. VERDICT ON THE USER'S IDENTIFICATION
=====================================================================
- Ref. 139 is correct: Tangente, 35 mm, sapphire case back. NOMOS's current official page lists ref. 139 with caliber "DUW 4001": manual wind, 2.6 mm high, 10½''' (23.3 mm), up to 53 h, 17 jewels, "NOMOS swing system and DUW regulation system". https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/tangente/tangente-139 [HIGH]
- The official ref. 139 case-back render (file named 0139_Tangente_GB_DUW_4001_…, uploaded 28 Apr 2025 per its CDN timestamp) shows a DUW 4001 engraved "N° 00535". https://cdn.nomos-glashuette.com/img/f=scale-down,w=2560,bg=gray,sf=png/webshop/a7/be/4e/1745849414/0139_Tangente_GB_DUW_4001_2d-back-masked_1.jpg [HIGH]
- Before the change, ref. 139 used caliber Alpha (43 h).
  - Wempe and MR Porter listings say "Alpha, 43 hours". https://www.wempe.com/en-int/watches/nomos-glashuette/tangente/tangente-139 and https://www.mrporter.com/en-us/mens/product/nomos-glashuette/luxury-watches/dress-watches/tangente-hand-wound-stainless-steel-ref-139/1647597336477236 [HIGH]
  - A calibercorner commenter bought a ref. 139 in 2023 and found an Alpha in it. https://calibercorner.com/nomos-caliber-a-alpha/ [MEDIUM]
- Date of the switch: no official announcement found. Inferred to be about 2025 [LOW-MEDIUM]. Evidence:
  - CDN timestamps on NOMOS images: Apr/Jun 2025.
  - A NOMOS X post about "Ludwig … DUW 4001, 53-hour" dated 3 Sep 2025 (from the tweet ID): https://x.com/nomosglashuette/status/1963241236606644364
  - Monochrome, 13 Nov 2025, describes DUW 4001 as an "evolution of the Calibre Alpha" (itself Peseux 7001-based) with the swing system and 53 h: https://monochrome-watches.com/2025-nomos-tangente-gold-neomatik-and-nomos-ludwig-gold-introducing/
- Transitional pieces exist. In April 2023 NOMOS reportedly said it was "converting all our hand-wound models from Triovis fine regulation to DUW regulation system" (calibercorner comment, URL above) [LOW-MEDIUM].
- History before 2005: Tangente series 1 used a Peseux 7001 reworked as "NOMOS 1 T"; Alpha came from 2005. https://wiki.grail-watch.com/index.php/Nomos_Tangente [MEDIUM]. Whether ref. 139 existed then is unknown.
- How to tell them apart from the back:
  - DUW 4001: plate engraved "DUW 4001 / N° xxxxx / NOMOS GLASHÜTTE / DEUTSCHE UHRENWERKE" plus "17 RUBINE"; blue hairspring; no regulator screw.
  - Alpha: "17 STEINE" plus the NOMOS logo, an "α"-prefixed serial, a silver (non-blue) hairspring, and a screw-adjusted triovis regulator.
  - Both are visible in the official renders (sec. 3) [HIGH].

=====================================================================
1. DUW 4001: SPECIFICATIONS
=====================================================================
Official NOMOS pages:
- EN: https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/caliber/duw-4001-duw-4001
- EN feature page: https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/watchmaking/nomos-movements/miracle-movements/duw-4001
- DE: https://nomos-glashuette.com/de/caliber/duw-4001-duw-4001

- Name: DUW = Deutsche Uhrenwerke NOMOS Glashütte. "DEUTSCHE UHRENWERKE" is engraved on the plate. [HIGH]
- Type: in-house manual-wind caliber ("Manufakturkaliber mit Handaufzug"). [HIGH]
- Diameter: 23.3 mm (10½ lignes). [HIGH]
- Height: 2.6 mm. [HIGH]
- Jewels: 17 ("17 Rubine"). [HIGH]
- Power reserve: "up to 53 hours" (EN) / "ca. 53 Stunden" (DE). [HIGH]
  - Conflict: WatchBase's data table reportedly says 43 h while its own text says about 53 h (seen in search snippets; the site returned 403 to direct fetch). Treat 43 h as an Alpha carry-over error.
- Frequency: 21,600 A/h = 3 Hz (6 beats/s). Not stated on the NOMOS pages. [MEDIUM-HIGH]
  - Deployant, 25 Jul 2026: https://deployant.com/new-nomos-glashutte-tetra-27-white-and-tetra-27-duo/
  - Monochrome "3Hz" (URL above).
- Functions: central hours and minutes, small seconds at 6, stop-seconds (hacking). [HIGH]
- Lineage: evolution of Alpha; same dimensions and layout. [HIGH for layout, from my own comparison of the two official renders: jewel and screw positions agree within ±0.4 mm]
  - Alpha 43 h vs DUW 4001 53 h.
  - Calibercorner (URL above) and Monochrome (URL above) agree.
- Plate architecture: "Glashütte three-quarter plate", described as spanning "both the mainspring barrel and gear train". [HIGH] https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/watchmaking/watchmaking-craft/nomos-and-glashutte/designation-of-origin
- "Glashütte stopwork" (German: "Glashütter Gesperr", which actually means the click work):
  - NOMOS describes it as a specially shaped ratchet/click with "an elongated ratchet spring for particularly smooth winding" (designation-of-origin URL above). [MEDIUM]
  - It is a click with a long curved spring, not a Geneva-style stop.
- Escapement: "NOMOS swing system", NOMOS's in-house Swiss-lever-type escapement, launched at Baselworld 2014. [HIGH]
  - Parts: balance, blue balance spring, escape wheel, pallet fork with 2 synthetic-ruby pallet stones fixed with shellac.
  - Balances are milled for poise with a laser and sorted into 40 classes to match their springs.
  - https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/watchmaking/watchmaking-craft/research-and-development/the-nomos-swing-system
- Balance (from the official component photo below): [HIGH visual]
  - Gold-coloured.
  - 3 tapered arms that flare into the rim.
  - Smooth screwless rim with small poising holes and recesses on its edge.
  - Double roller with a red D-shaped impulse jewel.
  - Material: unknown (not stated).
  - Photo: https://cdn.nomos-glashuette.com/img/f=scale-down,w=1024/cms/content/testing/glashuette/Unruhspirale_Swing_System_sa_PR-1.jpg
- Hairspring: in-house, "blue balance spring" (EN) / "gebläute Unruhspirale" (DE). [HIGH]
  - Monochrome calls it "tempered 'blue'". Whether it is heat-blued or coated: unknown. [LOW]
  - Flat spiral; ESTIMATE about 12 coils, counted from the NOMOS photo.
  - Alloy: unknown.
- Escape wheel (official photo): steel, skeletonised, 4 straight spokes (two crossing bars), club teeth. [HIGH visual]
  - Tooth count: not published (unknown).
  - ESTIMATE: 15 teeth. Reason: standard for a Swiss lever, and the photo is consistent.
  - Photo: https://cdn.nomos-glashuette.com/img/f=scale-down,w=1024/cms/content/testing/glashuette/Unruh_Ankerrad_Anker_PR.jpg
- Pallet fork (same photo): skeletonised steel, Y/T shape, two pink rectangular ruby stones at the arm ends, fork with horns and dart. [HIGH visual]
- Regulation: "DUW regulation system", with "Feinregulierung in sechs Lagen" (adjusted in six positions). [HIGH]
  - Glossary: https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/glossary/duw-regulation-system
  - No regulating screw.
  - The regulator and the stud carrier are each mounted on a ring on the balance cock.
  - The regulator carries the "balance spring key" (two pins with the spring running between them).
  - The stud carrier holds the outer end of the spring.
  - Both are moved with spiral pliers from above; the key and stud can each be turned on their own axes to centre the spring.
  - Not a swan neck: NOMOS reserves the swan neck for DUW 1001/2002 (Lambda/Lux).
  - Conflict: calibercorner calls the DUW 4001 regulator "ETACHRON". This is most likely a misidentification of the visually similar ring-type DUW system; NOMOS's own name is "DUW regulation system". [MEDIUM]
- Shock protection: not stated by NOMOS (unknown).
  - The photo shows a gold-coloured lyre-shaped spring over the balance cap jewel.
  - Calibercorner says Alpha uses Incabloc [LOW-MEDIUM].
  - ESTIMATE: Incabloc-type lyre on the upper balance jewel.
- Finishing (official list): [HIGH]
  - Rhodium-plated surfaces with Glashütte ribbing and NOMOS perlage.
  - Tempered blue screws.
  - Ratchet and crown wheel with Glashütte sunburst ("Sperr- und Kronrad mit Glashütter Sonnenschliff").
  - Deployant (URL above) calls the ratchet a "double-sunburst ratchet wheel": an outer toothed ring and an inner raised disc, each sunburst-brushed. This matches the photo.
- Gold chatons: none. Jewels are set directly into the plate, in polished sinks. [HIGH visual]
  - NOMOS uses screwed gold chatons only in Atelier calibers (designation-of-origin URL above).
- Number of parts: unknown (not published anywhere I found).
- Mainspring and barrel dimensions: unknown.
- Variant to avoid: "DUW 4001 Duo" is the 2-hand version with a shortened seconds arbor and no hack. Not used in ref. 139, which has small seconds. https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-us/caliber/duw-4001-duo-duw-4001-duo [HIGH]

2. LAYOUT FROM THE BACK: MEASURED POSITIONS
=====================================================================
Method:
- Source: NOMOS's official 2560 px DUW 4001 back render, https://cdn.nomos-glashuette.com/img/f=scale-down,w=2560/webshop/99/fe/4e/1750326818/DUW_4001_front_f7.jpg
- I fitted a circle to the plate edge: centre at pixel (1276.4, 1205.2), radius 633.9 px, 23.3 mm diameter, giving 54.41 px/mm. The centre-wheel jewel lies exactly at this centre, which validates the fit.
- The render is oriented exactly as in the watch. Cross-checked against the ref. 139 case-back render: same orientation, movement concentric with the case and not rotated; the balance measures (6.32, −0.67) there vs (6.27, −0.79) on the bare movement.
- Accuracy: about ±0.15 mm for jewels, about ±0.3 mm for other features.
- All positions below are measurements from the photo (MEASURED). Part identifications follow standard practice; confidence is noted on each.

Train (upper jewels; 17 jewels = 6 upper + 6 lower + 2 caps + 2 pallet stones + 1 impulse):
- Centre (2nd) wheel arbor: (0.00, 0.00).
  - A gilt toothed wheel with tip radius about 3.3–3.4 mm (tip diameter about 6.6–6.8 mm) shows through plate openings. Its curvature centre is at the movement centre, so it is the centre wheel [MEDIUM].
- Third wheel jewel: (−1.66, −3.24); r 3.65; clock about 6:54. [MEDIUM]
- Fourth wheel (small seconds arbor): (+0.09, −6.50); r 6.50; clock 6:00. [HIGH; it sits at 6 o'clock as the subdial requires]
- Escape wheel jewel (in the 3/4 plate): about (+3.2, −6.2) ±0.2; r about 7.0; clock about 5:05. [MEDIUM]
- Pallet arbor jewel (small pallet bridge under the balance, perlage-finished): (+4.53, −3.75); r 5.88; clock about 4:19. [MEDIUM]
- Balance staff: (+6.27, −0.79); r 6.32; clock about 3:14. [HIGH]
- Escape → pallet → balance are collinear within about 2°: a straight-line lever. [MEDIUM-HIGH]
  - Centre distances: escape–pallet 2.79 mm; pallet–balance 3.43 mm.
  - One pallet stone is visible at about (+3.40, −3.08), roughly 30° off the line of centres, which is consistent with this geometry.
- Balance rim outer diameter: about 9.3 mm (gold ring peaks at 4.5–4.65 mm radius; rim about 0.3 mm wide). [MEDIUM]
- Hairspring visible diameter: ESTIMATE about 4–4.5 mm.

Winding and keyless works:
- Ratchet wheel / barrel arbor: centre (−2.25, +5.61); r 6.04; clock about 11:16. [HIGH position]
  - Tip diameter about 8.2 mm.
  - Held by one large slotted steel screw, head about 2 mm.
  - The barrel sits beneath it under the 3/4 plate.
- Crown wheel: centre (−5.91, +0.05); r 5.91; clock 9:00. [HIGH position]
  - Tip diameter about 5.75 mm.
  - Covered by a plain polished disc about 4.0 mm in diameter, fixed by 2 small slotted screws at (−5.91, +1.04) and (−5.91, −0.99).
  - The crown wheel sits in a recess in the plate and meshes with the ratchet towards 1–2 o'clock of the crown wheel.
- Stem axis: horizontal at y ≈ 0, entering from the left (9 o'clock in back view). [HIGH]
  - A vertical slot window about 0.6 × 2.9 mm at x −9.25 to −8.6, y −1.4 to +1.5 exposes the winding pinion meshing with the crown wheel [MEDIUM on identification].
- Click (Glashütte-style pawl with long curved spring), at about 10–11 o'clock between the ratchet and the plate edge: [MEDIUM]
  - Pawl tip engages the ratchet at about (−5.2, +7.8).
  - Click/plate screw in an elongated polished recess at (−8.2, +6.0).
  - Curved spring arm runs along the edge from about 10 o'clock to 11:30, r about 9–11 mm.
- Hidden from the back (dial side, unknown geometry): setting lever, yoke, stop-seconds lever, motion works.

Bridges:
- One large 3/4 plate covers barrel and train.
- Separate balance cock: [HIGH visual]
  - Foot fills the sector from about 1:00 to 2:45, from r about 7 mm out to the edge, held by blued screw #2.
  - The arm dog-legs left, then runs as a strip about 1 mm wide at x ≈ 3.4–4.3 mm from y ≈ +4.7 down to +0.1.
  - It then turns right into a ring housing about 3.4 mm in diameter, centred on the balance.
  - The housing holds the DUW regulator ring and the stud-carrier ring.
  - The regulator (index) arm points towards about 7 o'clock of the balance; its gold-coloured "spring key" block is at about (+5.4, −3.6).
  - The stud-carrier arm points towards about 4:30; the stud block is at about (+8.4, −2.9).
- Small pallet bridge under the balance (see the pallet arbor jewel above).
- The Glashütte ribbing runs continuously across both the plate and the cock.

Blued screws: 5 in total, all slotted, heads about 1.5 mm in diameter. [HIGH positions]
- #1 (+3.85, +8.24): r 9.10, clock 12:50, on the plate.
- #2 (+7.21, +5.46): r 9.05, clock 1:46, the balance-cock screw.
- #3 (−8.72, +3.31): r 9.33, clock 9:42, on the plate.
- #4 (−8.13, −3.69): r 8.93, clock 8:11, on the plate.
- #5 (+6.72, −6.81): r 9.57, clock 4:31, on the plate.
- The same 5 positions appear on the Alpha render.

Plain steel (rhodium/polished) screws, besides the ratchet screw and the 2 crown-disc screws: [MEDIUM]
- (−10.0, −1.38): clock 8:44.
- (−8.29, −6.62): clock 7:43.
- (+6.45, +8.13): clock 1:17, in a round recess at the junction of cock foot and plate.
- (+0.85, +10.85): 12 o'clock edge, half-moon casing screw.
- Edge notches with holes for casing clamps: (+2.7, +10.6) at 12:29 and (−1.9, −10.66) at 6:20. In the cased render, a slotted steel screw sits in the 6:20 notch.

Engravings on the DUW 4001 (official renders): [HIGH for text; positions MEASURED ±0.3 mm]
- Main block: gold-filled, upright, uppercase sans-serif (DIN-like), cap height about 0.65 mm, all lines left-aligned at x ≈ −5.9:
  - "DUW 4001" at y ≈ −5.15.
  - "N° 00001" at y ≈ −6.2. This is the movement serial; the ref. 139 render shows "N° 00535".
  - "NOMOS GLASHÜTTE" at y ≈ −7.7.
  - "DEUTSCHE UHRENWERKE" at y ≈ −8.7, extending to x ≈ +5.4.
- "17 RUBINE": gold-filled, vertical along the left edge at x ≈ −10.3, y from about +4.2 down to 0. Rotated 90° clockwise, reads top-to-bottom, "17" at the top.
- "MADE IN" / "GERMANY": unfilled grey engraving, two lines, vertical near the right edge (x ≈ +10.1 to +11.3, y ≈ +0.6 to +3.2), right of the balance. Rotated 90° counter-clockwise, reads bottom-to-top; "GERMANY" is the line nearer the edge.
- No "SWING SYSTEM" or "ADJUSTED … POSITIONS" text appears in the render.
- I found no source for "NOMOS GLASHÜTTE/SA" on the plate (unverified, probably not on current movements).

Finish geometry:
- Glashütte ribbing: broad straight diagonal bands rising to the right in back view at about 30° ±5°. Band pitch is an ESTIMATE of about 2.3 mm (fine brushed texture within each band).
- NOMOS perlage: visible on the sunk floor under the balance and on the pallet bridge.
- Polished chamfers on plate cut-outs and cock edges; polished jewel sinks.

Rotation directions for animation (derived from the gear meshes): [HIGH logic]
- In back view, the centre wheel turns counter-clockwise at 1 rev/h (on the dial the minute hand turns clockwise).
- Third wheel: clockwise.
- Fourth wheel: counter-clockwise at 1 rpm.
- Escape wheel: clockwise.
- Barrel: clockwise, slow.
- Ratchet while winding: clockwise.
- Crown wheel while winding: counter-clockwise.
- Balance: 3 Hz. ESTIMATE amplitude about 270–300° (generic value).

ESTIMATE: self-consistent gear train (none of this is published; it is fitted to the measured centre distances and the 21,600 A/h rate)
- Ratchet and crown wheel, measured from the photo using an angular FFT of the tooth pattern:
  - Ratchet about 58 teeth, tip diameter about 8.2 mm.
  - Crown wheel about 40 teeth (±2), tip diameter about 5.75 mm.
  - Both imply module about 0.136 mm. Predicted centre distance 6.7 mm vs 6.68 mm measured, which supports these counts. [LOW-MEDIUM]
- Centre wheel 80 / third pinion 10 (×8): gives a 3.65 mm centre distance and a predicted centre-wheel tip diameter of 6.65 mm, which matches the photo.
- Third wheel 75 / fourth pinion 10 (×7.5): 3.70 mm centre distance. Product 60, so the fourth wheel turns 1 rpm.
- Fourth wheel 84 / escape pinion 7 (×12): 3.2 mm centre distance. With a 15-tooth escape wheel this gives 12 rpm, which is 21,600 A/h.
- Escape wheel tip diameter about 4.8 mm, from R = d·cos 30° = 2.79 × 0.866.
- Barrel about 84 teeth / centre pinion 12 (×7): 6.04 mm centre distance; barrel diameter about 10.6–11 mm; about 7.6 mainspring turns for 53 h.
- Motion works (generic): cannon pinion 10 / minute wheel 30, minute pinion 8 / hour wheel 32 (12:1).
- Vertical stack (ESTIMATE, low confidence), total 2.6 mm:
  - Pillar plate about 1.0 mm (motion works recessed on the dial side).
  - Train level about 0.8 mm.
  - 3/4 plate about 0.5 mm.
  - Ratchet and crown wheel about 0.3 mm on top.
  - Balance cock and shock setting are the highest points.
