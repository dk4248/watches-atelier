NOMOS GLASHÜTTE TANGENTE REF. 139: IDENTITY AND EXTERIOR SPECIFICATION REPORT
(Research date 2026-10-15. Confidence: H/M/L. Watchbase, MR PORTER, Wempe, Bucherer specs, Richter Phillips and Caliber Corner's main page returned HTTP 403. Their figures come only from search-result snippets and are marked as such.)

1. IDENTITY: THE USER'S DESCRIPTION IS CORRECT FOR A CURRENT WATCH, WITH ONE CAVEAT
==================================================================
- Official model name: "Tangente", Ref. 139. NOMOS does not add a size suffix for this model. Retailers and some NOMOS special editions call it "Tangente 35" (e.g. 139.S19/139.S20 "Tangente 35 Four Seasons"). [H] https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-us/tangente/tangente-139 ; https://teddybaldassarre.com/en-int/products/tangente ; search snippets for watchbase.com/nomos-glashutte/tangente/139-s19 and 139-s20
- Ref. 139 is the 35 mm hand-wound Tangente with a sapphire display back. Ref. 101 is the same watch with a solid steel back. [H] https://monochrome-watches.com/revisiting-the-most-classic-nomos-the-35mm-hand-wound-tangente-review-price/ ; https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/tangente/tangente-101
- Current caliber per NOMOS: DUW 4001, in-house, hand-wound. This is on the EN, US, DE, JP, HK and IL product pages, all fetched 2026-10. None of these pages uses the word "Alpha". [H] https://nomos-glashuette.com/de/tangente/tangente-139 ; https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-jp/tangente/tangente-139
- Caveat: the caliber changed during production. DUW 4001 is the renamed and upgraded Alpha.
  - Monochrome (Feb 2023) reviewed the 139 with "Calibre Alpha", 43 h, 21,600 vph, 17 jewels. [H]
  - Atelier de Griff (2019) reviewed the 139 with Alpha, 43 h, and states it has no Swing System. [H] https://atelierdegriff.com/2019/10/08/hands-on-nomos-tangente-35mm-ref-139/amp/
  - Monochrome (16 Oct 2025) says DUW 4001 "was previously known as the Alpha calibre ... now features the Nomos Swing System escapement ... 21,600vph". [H] https://monochrome-watches.com/first-look-nomos-tetra-origins-ochra-terra-salvia-basalt-duw-4001-specs-price-live-pics/
  - Monochrome (13 Nov 2025) calls it "an evolution of the Calibre Alpha ... in-house Swing system and an elongated power reserve of 53 hours ... 3Hz". [H] https://monochrome-watches.com/2025-nomos-tangente-gold-neomatik-and-nomos-ludwig-gold-introducing/
  - A NOMOS X post (~Sept 2025, date decoded from the post ID) already names the hand-wound caliber DUW 4001 with 53 h. [M] https://x.com/nomosglashuette/status/1963241236606644364
  - Caliber Corner (reader comment, not the site itself) says a 139 bought in 2023 "came with an alpha movement" but already had the newer regulator. It quotes NOMOS: "converting all our hand-wound models from Triovis fine regulation to DUW regulation system." [L-M] https://calibercorner.com/nomos-caliber-a-alpha/
  - Exact changeover date: unknown. ESTIMATE: 2024 to mid-2025. Any 139 made before about 2024 almost certainly has an Alpha.
- How to tell which one the user owns:

  | Feature | Alpha | DUW 4001 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Power reserve | up to 43 h | up to 53 h |
  | Balance spring | Nivarox 1A, silver colour | tempered blue (visible through the back) |
  | Regulator | Triovis | DUW regulation system |
  | Escapement | sourced from ETA | in-house NOMOS Swing System |

  The blue hairspring is the visual test for the 3D model. [M-H] Sources: https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-us/caliber/alpha-alpha ; https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/caliber/duw-4001-duw-4001 ; https://calibercorner.com/nomos-caliber-a-alpha/ ; https://reference.grail-watch.com/family/nomos-alpha/
- Production years of Ref. 139:
  - Start year unknown. It existed by Oct 2014 (owner post). [M] https://post.smzdm.com/p/196752/
  - The 35 mm Tangente has existed since 1992. It used a Peseux 7001-based "Nomos 1 T" until 2005 and the Alpha from 2005. [M] https://wiki.grail-watch.com/index.php/Nomos_Tangente ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomos_Glash%C3%BCtte
  - Still in production in 2026. [H]
  - ESTIMATE: the numeric ref "139" probably dates from the mid/late 2000s. Not verified.
- Other NOMOS models that use DUW 4001 (in case the user has a similar-looking ref). The list comes from NOMOS regional pages, via a search snippet [M]:
  - Tangente 101 (solid back) and 139
  - Tangente midnight blue 132/133
  - Tangente 33: 122/123, champagne 150/151, duo 120/127
  - Tangente 38: 164/165; 38 midnight blue 166/167
  - Ludwig 201/205, 33 and 38 variants
  - Orion 301/309, 38 and other variants

  - "Duo" refs use DUW 4001 Duo, which has no running seconds and no hack. https://watchbase.com/nomos-glashutte/caliber/duw-4001-duo (snippet)
- Special editions on the 139 base, so check the dial and back:
  - 139.S17 "70 Jahre Grundgesetz": back engraved "Die Würde des Menschen ist unantastbar – 70 Jahre Grundgesetz"
  - 139.S19 / 139.S20 Four Seasons

2. CALIBER DUW 4001: OFFICIAL HEADLINE DATA (detail belongs to the movement subagent)
==================================================================
- Diameter 10½ lignes = 23.3 mm; height 2.6 mm; 17 jewels; power reserve up to 53 h; stop-seconds; adjusted in 6 positions. [H] https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/caliber/duw-4001-duw-4001
- Escapement and regulation: "NOMOS swing system" escapement, "blue balance spring", "DUW regulation system". [H] same page
- Finishing: Glashütte three-quarter plate, Glashütte stopwork, tempered blue screws, rhodium-plated surfaces with Glashütte ribbing and NOMOS perlage, ratchet and crown wheels with Glashütte sunburst. [H] same page
- Frequency: 21,600 vph (3 Hz). [H] Monochrome 2025 x2. Not stated on the NOMOS page.
- Conflicts:
  - Watchbase's table lists 43 h; NOMOS says 53 h. Use 53 h. Search snippet.
  - Monochrome's Oct 2025 article says 43 h; a reader comment and NOMOS say 53 h.
- Alpha for comparison: 23.3 × 2.6 mm, 17 jewels, up to 43 h, same finishing list. NOMOS still has the page: https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-us/caliber/alpha-alpha [H]
- Lineage: Alpha is based on the Peseux 7001. Unreliable source: watchscanning.com quotes the Alpha at 26.0 mm and a "hand-engraved balance cock". Both are wrong; do not use it for numbers.

3. CASE (current official specification unless noted)
==================================================================
- Diameter: 35.0 mm, without crown. [H] NOMOS product pages
- Height: 6.6 mm (139, sapphire back). [H] NOMOS EN/US/DE/JP/HK pages
  - The 101 with solid back is 6.2 mm, so the display back adds about 0.4 mm. [H] https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/tangente/tangente-101
  - Conflicts: Watchbase lists the 139 at 6.2 mm (snippet; probably a copy of the 101 figure). Atelier de Griff says 6.7 mm. watchscanning says 6.8 mm. Use 6.6 mm.
- Lug-to-lug ("Anstoßaußenmaß"): 45.0 mm. [H] NOMOS DE page
- Lug width: 18 mm. [H] NOMOS pages
- Material: stainless steel 316L. NOMOS says "316 L stainless steel for all cases and buckles". [H] https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/on-our-watches/in-general
- Finish: polished. Monochrome spec: "stainless steel case, polished". Escapement Magazine: "highly polished 35mm steel case". [M-H] https://escapementmagazine.com/articles/nomos-glashutte-tangente/
- Form:
  - "Flattened cylinder" with flat vertical sides; lugs are "simple straight steel bands, attached directly to the case". [H] Monochrome 2023
  - Lugs are drilled through for spring-bar access: "(drilled) lug design" (Atelier de Griff); "holey lugs" (Analog:Shift on the 38 mm Tangente 165). [M] https://www.analogshift.com/products/nomos-tangente-as07072
  - Grail wiki says "screwed bars"; this conflicts with the drilled-lug evidence. [L] https://wiki.grail-watch.com/index.php/Nomos_Tangente
- Construction: sources conflict.
  - "Bipartite" (two-piece): Teddy Baldassarre spec. [M] https://teddybaldassarre.com/en-int/products/tangente
  - "Tripartite": Atelier de Griff. [L-M]
  - Back attachment: grail wiki says "pressed caseback" (Tangente) and "Press-in bottom" (Tangente Datum, same case family). [L-M] https://wiki.grail-watch.com/index.php/Nomos_Tangente_Datum
  - Screw count or threading: unknown. ESTIMATE: snap/press-fit steel back ring holding the sapphire, flush with the case flank, no visible screws. This is consistent with "press-in", with 3–5 atm rating, and with the 0.4 mm height difference.
- Front crystal: flat sapphire. Monochrome spec says "flat sapphire crystal". [H for flat] AR coating on the 139 is unconfirmed; watchscanning claims underside AR on newer pieces. [L]
- Case back: sapphire display back in a steel ring.
  - NOMOS: "Saphirglasboden"; movement "sichtbar durch den Saphirglasboden". [H]
  - Dealer: "Boden Edelstahl graviert mit Saphirglas Sichtfenster" (engraved steel back with a sapphire window). [M] Wristler search snippet (wristler.eu/watches/228489-nomos-tangente)
- Water resistance: CONFLICT, which depends on the year.
  - Current NOMOS: 5 atm, "splash-proof". [H]
  - Older and retail specs: 3 atm / 30 m. Monochrome 2023 says 30 m; Exquisite Timepieces says 3 ATM; grail wiki says 3 atm. [H that older material says 3 atm]
  - ESTIMATE: rating raised to 5 atm at about the time of the DUW 4001 switch. Not confirmed.
- Crown:
  - Push-pull, not screw-down. Screw-down crowns are listed only for Ahoi, Tangente Sport and Club Sport. [H] NOMOS FAQ
  - Small, knurled, "couldn't be simpler" (Monochrome). [M]
  - Signed or not for the 139: unconfirmed. Analog:Shift lists "Signed crown" on a 1990s gold 35 mm Tangente and on a steel Tangente 38 ref 165. Teddy Baldassarre describes the 2Date crown as having the NOMOS logo. [L-M for the 139]
  - Crown dimensions: unknown. ESTIMATE: about 4.5–5.0 mm diameter, about 2.0–2.5 mm protrusion, fine straight knurling, flat end face with the NOMOS logotype engraved. Reasoning: proportions in product photos relative to 35 mm, and a slim 2.6 mm movement whose stem sits about 1.3 mm above the pillar plate.

4. DIAL AND HANDS
==================================================================
- Dial: galvanized, white silver-plated ("galvanisiert, weiß versilbert"). [H] NOMOS
  - Monochrome adds "slightly grained". [M]
  - Atelier de Griff: "not plain white, and has a hint of silver"; texture differs between the main dial and the subdial. [M]
- Functions: hours, minutes, small seconds at 6 o'clock. No date and no power-reserve indicator. [H] NOMOS
- Small seconds: snailed (Monochrome); recessed (MR PORTER snippet, watchscanning). Sits at 6, fairly close to the centre; readers comment it is "too high up". [M]
- Numerals: five Arabic numerals in Bauhaus-era typography (NOMOS). [H]
  - They mark the even hours (12, 2, 4, 8, 10); 6 is taken by the subdial. Odd hours have thin batons (Escapement Magazine). [M-H]
  - Printed black. Monochrome says "positioned radially (even though tilted in the bottom half)". [M]
- Dial text:
  - "NOMOS" and "Glashütte". Current dials no longer carry "/SA": Monochrome 2023 says "The Glashütte logo is not accompanied by the SA mention anymore." [M]
  - NOMOS says the SA affix was dropped from the logo in 2005. Search snippet of the NOMOS FAQ. [M]
  - watchscanning still claims "GLASHUTTE/SA". [L] Older dials may differ.
  - Exact text positions: unconfirmed. ESTIMATE: "NOMOS" under 12, "Glashütte" in the lower half above or near the subdial.
- Minute track: printed black minute scale (grail wiki). [M] Exact geometry unknown.
- Hands: steel, tempered blue ("temperaturgebläut"), heated to 290 °C, "cornflower blue". Hour and minute hands are "supremely slender", unchanged since 1992; the seconds hand is also blued. [H] NOMOS ; Escapement Magazine
- Dimensions: unknown, except where marked ESTIMATE.
  - ESTIMATE dial diameter: about 32 mm visible. Case 35 mm minus a thin polished bezel lip.
  - ESTIMATE subdial: about 8–9 mm diameter, centre about 6–7 mm below the dial centre. The seconds wheel arbor must lie within the 23.3 mm movement (radius 11.65 mm), and photos show the subdial well inside the 4/8 numerals.
  - ESTIMATE hand lengths: minute about 14.5–15 mm (to the minute track), hour about 9.5–10.5 mm, seconds about 3.5–4 mm.

8. BOTTOM LINE FOR THE 3D BUILD
==================================================================
- "Tangente ref 139 with DUW 4001" is a valid, current NOMOS configuration. NOMOS made it, and the user's memory is right for a watch made from about 2025 on.
- If the user's watch is pre-2024, the movement is the Alpha. Same plates and dimensions, but:
  - non-blue (Nivarox) hairspring
  - Triovis regulator
  - ETA-sourced escapement
  - 43 h power reserve
- Ask the user to check whether the hairspring through the back is blue (DUW 4001) or silver (Alpha).
- Model parameters: 35.0 Ø × 6.6 mm, 45.0 mm lug-to-lug, 18 mm lugs, polished 316L, flat sapphire front, press-fit sapphire back, 5 atm (current), 23.3 × 2.6 mm movement at 21,600 vph.
