FACT-CHECK: case and dial section, NOMOS Tangente ref. 139 (build spec v1.0)
Checked 2026-10-15. I worked only from primary sources: the official NOMOS 139, 101 and DE 139 pages, plus official NOMOS images that I downloaded and measured myself. Reviews and forums were used only to cross-check.

Nothing in the case and dial area is clearly wrong. Every dimension NOMOS publishes holds up. The two values that should be reconsidered are the subdial offset (I measure 6.55, the spec chose 6.40) and the crown flute count (I estimate about 22, the spec says 24, both low confidence).

My pixel scale: case circle fitted on the official 1600 px front render gives R = 360.3 px = 17.5 mm, so 20.59 px/mm. The main dial centre and the hour-hand hub agree to within 1 px.
