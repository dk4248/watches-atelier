Method:
- **Centre.** The case-outline circle fit (rms 0.24 px), the hub pin and the 60 minute ticks agree within 0.1 px.
- **Scale.** Lug-to-lug 47.3 gives 23.49 px/mm. Case silhouette Ø 38.5 gives 23.53. I used **23.50**, uncertain to about ±0.3 %.
- **Line widths.** Measured as FWHM across the stroke. Uncertainty about ±0.02.
- **Positions.** Uncertainty about ±0.04 at r < 17.

The render shows **10:12:00** consistently (hour 305.8°, minute 72.0°, seconds at 0), and the date on the dial is **3**.

3.5 THE UPDATE DATE RING: how it works visually [MEAS H; mechanism description REV H]
Construction:
- The numerals **1–31 are printed on the dial**, which is fixed.
- Between consecutive numerals the dial has **31 stadium-shaped cut-outs** (Worn & Wound calls them oval windows). They go right through the dial; the slot walls are visible in the macro shots.
- Under the dial a **white date disc** rotates (Time+Watches and Monochrome both describe a disc beneath the dial). It shows light grey through every slot except two, where **two red markers** printed on the disc fill the slots on either side of the current date.
- The whole month stays visible. Nothing is printed on the disc except the markers.

Geometry (date angle step **Δ = 360/31 = 11.6129°**):
- **Numeral d** is centred at clock angle **θ = (d mod 31)·Δ**:
  - **"31" sits exactly at 12 o'clock**, "1" at 11.61°, "15" at 174.19°, "16" at 185.81°.
  - Measured centres agree within ±0.27°. Since 15.5·Δ = 180°, the 6 o'clock axis falls between 15 and 16.
- **Slot k** (between dates k and k+1) is centred at **(k + ½)·Δ**: 5.81°, 17.42°, … 180.00° (between 15 and 16), … 354.19°.
- Track: slots and numerals share one circle, **r_track ≈ 16.55**.
- Slot:

Motion:
- The disc **rotates clockwise (seen from the dial) by Δ = 11.613° per day**, carrying the marker pair from one date to the next. From 31 to 1 it is the same single step.
- It is fixed during the day and advances around midnight. The exact switching profile (instant jump vs creep) is not published; this is for the movement report. Monochrome says date correction is blocked for about 90 min around midnight, roughly 23:15–00:45. ESTIMATE for the replica: hold, then step over about 0.3 s at 00:00.
- Quick-set in **both directions** via the crown [OFF/REV H]. Months shorter than 31 days need manual correction (not a perpetual).
- three.js: model the markers on the disc at ±Δ/2 around 12 o'clock, then `rotor.rotation.z = −(D mod 31)·Δ` (CCW-positive convention).
