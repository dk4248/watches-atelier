F. NET CORRECTIONS TO APPLY

1. "17 RUBINE" and "MADE IN / GERMANY" are arc text:
   - "17 RUBINE": r 10.6, from about 157° to 179° CCW from +x in back view.
   - "MADE IN" at about r 10.5, "GERMANY" at about r 11.2, both near 3 o'clock in back view.
   - Neither is straight vertical text.
2. Ribbing pitch is about 2.0, not 2.3.
3. The hairspring is NOMOS-assembled; whether NOMOS makes it is unconfirmed (2014: outsourced to a German supplier).
4. The Alpha toggle must also change the plate edge notch at 3 o'clock, the Triovis cock-end parts and the "α" serial on the cock.
5. The cock-end ring assembly's outer diameter is about 4.0.
6. The regulator arm points to about 6:30, not 7:00.
7. Frequency (21,600) and lift angle (50°) come from reviews and the ETA 7001, not from NOMOS. Jewel count, 53 h, 23.3 × 2.6, the finishing list and the engraving texts are confirmed from NOMOS.
