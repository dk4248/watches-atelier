NOMOS TANGENTE REF. 139: VISUAL DESIGN BRIEF FOR A TO-SCALE MODEL (dial, hands, case)

Status: the reference and caliber you were given are correct. Most of the measurements below come from my own pixel analysis of NOMOS's official product photos. Where a value is not published, it is marked ESTIMATE and the reasoning is given.

Conventions: all values are in mm. Positions are given from the dial centre, with the angle measured clockwise from 12 and r = radius.

======================================================================
0. IDENTITY CHECK
======================================================================
- Ref. 139 is the Tangente 35 with a sapphire caseback. Case Ø 35.0, height 6.6, lug-to-lug 45.0, lug width 18. Caliber DUW 4001, hand-wound, 17 jewels, movement Ø 23.3 (10½ lines), movement height 2.6. [H]
  https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-us/tangente/tangente-139 ; https://nomos-glashuette.com/de/tangente/tangente-139 ; https://nomos-glashuette.com/en/caliber/duw-4001-duw-4001
- DUW 4001 is the current name of the "Alpha" caliber. [M-H] NOMOS's regional 139 page says "formerly Alpha" (seen only in a search snippet of https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-il/tangente/tangente-139). Watchscanning also writes "Alpha caliber (DUW 4001)". The caliber page itself does not say it.
- Sister model ref. 101 has a solid steel back. It is 6.2 high with otherwise the same case. https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-us/tangente/tangente-101 [H]
- Conflicts:
  - Height: NOMOS gives 6.6 [H]. Atelier de Griff gives 6.7 (https://atelierdegriff.com/2019/10/08/hands-on-nomos-tangente-35mm-ref-139/). Watchscanning gives 6.8 and lug-to-lug 44 (https://www.watchscanning.com/guides/how-to-spot-fake-nomos-tangente/, low reliability). Wempe's product text says 6.2, which is the ref. 101 figure (search snippet only; the page returned 403).
  - Power reserve: NOMOS says 53 h now. Older sources (Wempe, Atelier de Griff 2019) say 43 h, so it may differ by production year.

2. DIAL LAYOUT (from the front image unless noted)
======================================================================
Overall
- Visible dial radius is 15.75 (Ø about 31.5). From 15.75 to 16.15 there is a dark band, which is the case's inner wall seen through the crystal.
- The dial is flat, except for a recessed small-seconds area. [M-H]

Arabic numerals
- Only at 12, 2, 4, 8 and 10. There is no 6; the subdial sits there. [H]
  NOMOS describes them as "five numerals with typography reminiscent of the Bauhaus era" (139 page).
- All numerals sit in the radial band r 10.0–13.45. Height is 3.24–3.39 and stroke about 0.25–0.30 (monoline). [H]
- Each numeral is rotated to lie along its radius. Their orientation differs between the top and bottom of the dial. [H, confirmed by measurement]
  - 12, 2, 10: the top of the numeral points outward. So the 2 is turned 60° clockwise and the 10 is turned 60° counter-clockwise.
  - 4, 8: the top of the numeral points toward the centre (base toward the rim). So the 4 is turned 60° counter-clockwise (120°−180°) and the 8 is turned 60° clockwise (240°−180°).
  - Evidence: the 4's closed triangle sits at the inner radius (r 10.26–12.52). The 8's smaller loop (0.65 mm²) is on the inner side and its larger loop (1.06 mm²) on the outer side. The close-up dial photo confirms this.
- Widths across the radius: "12" is 1.79 overall (the "1" glyph is 0.30 wide, the "2" is 1.28, with a gap of about 0.2). The "2" alone is 1.40, "4" 1.49, "8" 1.30, and "10" 1.78 (the "0" is 1.29).
- Proportions: very narrow, about 2.5:1 height to width.

Glyph shapes (Art-Deco/Bauhaus condensed, uniform stroke)
- "1": a plain stem with tiny serifs at top and foot.
- "2": the top is an arch whose left end drops vertically into a short serifed spur. A diagonal runs to a flat base, and the base's right end turns up into a short spur.
- "4": closed, flat-topped trapezoidal counter. The crossbar runs past the stem with a short spur/tail.
- "8": two stacked rounded-rectangle loops; the upper loop is smaller.
- "0": a pill shape with straight parallel sides.
- Origin: the design is based on a 1937 Weber & Baral Glashütte dial (also supplied to A. Lange & Söhne). Susanne Günther refined the typography for the 1992 launch. https://swisswatches-magazine.com/?p=104179 [M]
- Time+Tide describes "tall Arabic numeral font", "thin stick hands" and "long, thin lugs". https://timeandtidewatches.com/the-immortals-the-nomos-tangente-is-a-minimalist-masterclass-for-the-wrist/ [M]

Baton hour indices
- At 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11. They run radially along the same band as the numerals, r 10.0–13.45 (length about 3.4). [H]
- Width 0.19–0.21. [H]
- None at 6.

Minute track
- 60 radial ticks at r 14.15–14.95 (length 0.80), all the same length. [H]
- One-minute ticks are hairlines about 0.065–0.075 wide. The 12 five-minute ticks (including at 12 and 6) are about 0.20–0.21 wide, the same as the batons. [H]
- There are no rails or connecting circle (checked radially), no dots and no minute numerals. [H]
- Conflict: watchscanning calls it a "railroad-style" track. The official photos show no rails.

Printed text (all black print)
- "NOMOS": wide geometric sans capitals with circular O's. [H]
  - Baseline at y = +5.87, cap top at +6.76 (O height 0.90), overall width 7.46, centred.
  - Letter widths: N 0.85, O 0.94, M 1.58, O 0.94, S 0.77. Gaps about 0.60–0.68. Stroke about 0.15–0.19.
- "GLASHÜTTE": condensed, medium-weight sans with wide tracking. [H]
  - Baseline at +4.80, cap height about 0.64 (umlaut dots reach +5.52). Width 5.50, centred, about 0.64 per letter.
  - Older dials reportedly read "GLASHÜTTE/SA". This is from watchscanning only [L] and does not appear on the current dial.
- "MADE IN GERMANY": small light capitals set along an arc, outside the minute track, between the tick ends (14.95) and the dial edge (15.75). [H]
  - Letters span r 15.06–15.47, so cap height is about 0.40. The base of the letters is on the outer side and the tops point to the centre, so it reads upright at 6.
  - It spans 164.6°–194.9° (about 30°, arc length about 8.1 at r 15.25), centred on 6. The thick 30-minute tick sits just inside it, between "IN" and "GERMANY".
  - Stroke about 0.06–0.08.
- The dial carries no other text.

Small seconds
- Centre on the 6 o'clock axis, 6.50 below the dial centre. [H]
  This is a useful constraint for where the fourth-wheel arbor sits in the movement model.
- Recessed disc with its step edge at r about 4.6–4.9 (Ø about 9.5). [M]
- The surface has fine concentric circular grooves, visible in the close-up photo. [H]
  - ESTIMATE pitch about 0.14, measured from the close-up photo at about 45 px/mm.
- Twelve ticks, one every 5 s, at r 3.24–4.07 (length 0.83). [H]
  - Ticks at 0/15/30/45 s are about 0.20 wide; the others about 0.10.
  - There are no numerals. Watchscanning claims sub-dial numerals, which conflicts with the official photos; I treat that source as unreliable.
- Recess depth: unknown. ESTIMATE 0.1–0.2, since typical NOMOS recesses read as a shallow step.

Dial finish
- "galvanized, white silver-plated" (official) [H]. In German: "galvanisiert, weiß versilbert".
- The close-up shows a fine frosted grain (pixel noise about ±4/255). Atelier de Griff notes the subdial texture differs from the main dial. [M]

======================================================================
3. HANDS
======================================================================
Material
- "steel, tempered blue". Blued by heat ("heating to 290°C"), which NOMOS calls "cornflower blue" (139 page). [H]
- Flat (not curved), with polished edges per watchscanning [L-M]. No lume [H].

Hour hand
- Thin stick, tip at r 9.35–9.40, which stops about 0.65 short of the numerals and batons (inner edge 10.0). [H]
- Width about 0.40 near the hub, about 0.37 by r 8.5 (ink width 0.50 to 0.45). [M-H]
- Short pointed tip, tapering over the last 0.4–0.5. [M-H]
- No counterweight. [H]

Minute hand
- Stick, tip at r 14.70–14.75, which reaches about 2/3 into the minute ticks (14.15–14.95). [H]
- Width about 0.38 near the hub, tapering to about 0.34 at r 13–14.3. Pointed tip tapering over about 0.4. No counterweight. [M-H]
- Length ratio hour : minute = 0.64.

Central hub
- Blue, domed, stepped collets. Outer Ø about 2.2–2.3 (hour-hand collet); inner step Ø about 1.3–1.4 (minute-hand collet). [M]
- A polished steel pin/cap shows at the centre, Ø about 0.5–0.6. [M]

Seconds hand
- Blue stick about 0.28 wide. It reaches 3.95–4.0 from the subdial centre (about the outer end of the ticks), with a tail of 1.45–1.5. [H]
- Hub Ø about 1.25 with a light pin Ø about 0.3 at the centre. [M]

Thickness and height above the dial
- Unknown. ESTIMATE hand blade thickness 0.10–0.12 (typical blued steel). ESTIMATE heights above the dial: seconds about 0.2, hour about 0.3, minute about 0.5. These are standard stack clearances, not measured.

======================================================================
4. CASE, CRYSTAL, CROWN, LUGS, STRAP
======================================================================
Case and crystal
- 316L stainless steel, polished. NOMOS describes it as "Glashütte stainless steel 316 L … approx. 12% nickel". [H]
- Crystal is flat sapphire, flush with or slightly above the bezel; a domed crystal is listed as a counterfeit sign. Watchscanning [L-M]; the official side photo shows no dome [M-H].
- Front view, measured [M]:
  - Polished bezel top runs from r about 16.65 to 17.5 (about 0.85–1.0 wide), with an inner chamfer/crystal edge at about 16.3–16.6.
  - Glass opening Ø about 32.6–33.2.
- Unknown, ESTIMATE: crystal thickness about 1.0–1.2; dial true Ø about 32 (it must exceed the 31.5 visible); dial thickness about 0.4.

Side profile (official side photo, corrected for perspective)
- Construction, top to bottom: a thin polished bezel ring, then a straight-walled cylindrical middle case, then a chamfered display caseback ring. Atelier de Griff calls it three-part with "just a hint of a bezel". [M]
- ESTIMATE layer heights within the 6.6 total: bezel about 1.4, middle case about 3.2, caseback about 1.6 (including the sapphire). These are proportions from the photo, about ±10%.
- Caseback: sapphire display window in a steel ring engraved with NOMOS GLASHÜTTE, reference/serial number and the steel marking. [L-M] (watchscanning)

Crown
- Fluted crown with a scalloped edge and "NOMOS" engraved across its flat end face (visible in the side and three-quarter photos). [H]
  A dealer listing for ref. 135 also mentions the logo; a 1990s example is described as a "Signed crown" (https://analogshift.com/products/nomos-tangente-as04806).
- Push-pull, not screw-down. [M]
- Measured ESTIMATES:
  - Ø 5.0–5.4 (front about 5.0–5.37; side about 5.1–5.5).
  - Sticks out about 2.1 past the case edge (to r about 19.6).
  - About 24 flutes (one every ~15°), each about 0.2 deep.
  - Axis at about mid-height of the middle case (about 3.8 below the crystal top).

Lugs
- NOMOS: "The angular lugs are a key feature". Reviewers: "long, thin lugs", "Drilled lugs", "straight and wire-like".
- Front view, measured [M-H]:
  - Inner faces at ±9.0 (18 mm strap); outer faces at ±10.1–10.35. Each lug is about 1.1–1.3 wide seen from above.
  - Straight and parallel. Tips at ±22.3 from centre (official 45.0 lug-to-lug implies ±22.5).
  - They stick out about 7.7 beyond the case circle.
- Side profile, ESTIMATE from photo:
  - Each lug leaves the top of the middle case horizontally (top about 1.5 below the crystal top) for about 1.8–2.0 past the bezel edge.
  - It then bends down at about 45–50° for about 6, ending in a flat-cut tip that reaches slightly below the caseback plane.
  - The horizontal part is about 2.4 thick (vertically). It has a through-hole for the spring bar, Ø about 1.0, in the bent section.

Strap
- Horween Shell Cordovan, black, 18 mm (official). Tang buckle per a retailer listing. [M]
- Taper and buckle width are unknown. ESTIMATE 18→16.
